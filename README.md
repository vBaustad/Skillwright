<h1 align="center">Skillwright</h1>

<p align="center">
  <b>Your profession, leveled the smart way.</b><br>
  A profession leveling helper for <b>WoW Classic Era (1.15.x)</b>.
</p>

---

Skillwright shows you the **optimal craft path** from your current skill to 300 — what to
make right now, how many, and exactly which materials you still need. It reads your live
skill and **attaches to your profession window**, so the next step is always in front of you.

## Features

- **Now** — a clear *"make this next"* card: the recipe, how many more to craft, and the
  materials as item icons with have / need counts. Shift-click any item to link it in chat.
- **Steps** — the full path 1 → 300 split into trainer tiers, with your current step
  highlighted and finished tiers tucked away.
- **Shopping** — every material still needed, grouped by tier, green when covered and red
  when short.
- **Buy & Train in one click** — at a vendor, a **Buy** button purchases the mats it stocks
  that your next steps need; at a trainer, a **Train** button learns the recipes your path
  calls for.
- **Gathering routes** — Herbalism, Mining, Skinning and Fishing get zone-by-zone leveling
  routes instead of a craft path.
- **Follows your profession window** — attaches beside it and moves with it, or float it
  freely (toggle in settings).
- **Dashboard + minimap button** — every profession on your character in one place.

## Supported professions

All twelve. Craft paths for **Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Cooking, Enchanting, Engineering,
First Aid, Leatherworking** and **Tailoring**; gathering routes for **Herbalism, Mining,
Skinning** and **Fishing**.

## Usage

- Open a profession, or type **`/sw`** for the dashboard.
  - **`/sw guide`** — open the guide window directly.
  - **`/sw config`** — open settings.
- Switch view with the **Now / Steps / Shopping** tabs.
- Left-click the minimap button for the dashboard, right-click for settings.

## Notes

Material counts and item IDs are compiled from public leveling guides and verified where
possible — if something looks off, please [open an issue](https://github.com/vBaustad/Skillwright/issues).

Made for **WoW Classic Era**.
